Their absolute maximum input, output, and temperature

of the complete UE0085 module.

### 2.5 Module Power and Thermal Considerations

The module uses the following power architecture:

- VIN supplies the TPS61023 boost converter.
- TPS61023 generates the regulated 5 V rail.
- AP2112K-3.3 generates the 3.3 V rail from 5 V.
- TCAN1051HVD operates from the regulated 5 V supply.
- VIO selects either the 3.3 V or 5 V logic rail.
- Both regulated rails are accessible through the module headers.

The AP2112K power dissipation can be estimated as:

P_D ≈ (VIN(LDO) − VOUT) × IOUT

At a 5 V regulator input and 3.3 V output, the voltage
difference is approximately 1.7 V.

The available external current on the 3.3 V and 5 V rails
has not been independently qualified.

Do not exceed the thermal, electrical, or current limits
of any individual component.
